Explanatory note
This explanatory note relates to this Bill as introduced into Parliament.

Overview of Bill
The objects of this Bill are as follows—
(a) to amend the Lobbying of Government Officials Act 2011 (the Act) to—
      (i)    extend the scope of the Act to include a wider range of public officials, lobbyists and
             lobbying activities, and
      (ii) provide for public disclosure of lobbying activities, and
      (iii) provide that senior public officials must not engage in lobbying for 4 years after
             leaving office, and
      (iv) establish a Lobbying and Integrity Commission to administer the Act,
(b) to amend the Lobbying of Government Officials (Lobbyists Code of Conduct) Regulation
      2014 to implement a new Lobbyists Code of Conduct that reflects the proposed
      amendments to the Act.

Outline of provisions
Clause 1 sets out the name, also called the short title, of the proposed Act.
Clause 2 provides for the commencement of the proposed Act.

Schedule 1[12] inserts proposed Parts 1A, 1B and 1C to provide for the establishment of the
Lobbying and Integrity Commission (the Commission) and the Lobbying and Integrity Advisory
Board and to make provision for making and investigating complaints about lobbying. Schedule
1[4] and [22] make consequential amendments.
Schedule 1[13] substitutes Parts 2 and 3 to provide for a lobbying code of conduct applying to
lobbyists and public officials and for the registration of lobbyists. Schedule 1[3], [5], [8], [10] and
[14]–[16] make consequential amendments.
Schedule 1[17] substitutes Part 6 and inserts Parts 6A, 6B and 6C to provide for a lobbying
exclusion period, the disclosure by both lobbyists and senior public officials of lobbying activities,
further obligations of senior public officials and agencies and enforcement and investigative
functions of the Commission.
Schedule 1[18] requires the Commission to give a lobbyist written notice of an adverse decision
about the lobbyist and the Commission’s reasons for the decision.
Schedule 1[19] provides for the making of regulations.
Schedule 1[20] and [21] provide for the way that offences may be prosecuted.

                        (Lobbyists Code of Conduct) Regulation 2014

Conduct) Regulation 2014 to the Lobbying of Public Officials (Lobbying Code of Conduct)
Regulation 2014.
Schedule 2[2] makes an amendment consequential to the amendment of the name of the Act.
Schedule 2[3], [4] and [6]–[13] make amendments consequential on the amendments made by
Schedule 1[13] to the Lobbyists Register.
Schedule 2[5] makes an amendment to exempt lobbyists who are not prohibited donors and who
operate on a not-for-profit basis from the obligation to register.
Schedule 2[14] substitutes Schedule 1.
