        JUSTICE LEGISLATION AMENDMENT (MISCELLANEOUS) BILL (NO 2) 2026


    Schedule of amendment referred to in the Legislative Council's message of 24 September 2026.

No. 1   GRNS No. 5 [c2025-247I]
        Page 27, Schedule 1.20[1] and [2], lines 25–37. Omit all words on the lines. Insert instead—
        [1]    Section 3 Definitions
               Insert in alphabetical order in section 3(1)—
               Centre for Health Record Linkage means the group of persons referred to as the Centre
               for Health Record Linkage employed in the NSW Health Service.
        [2]    Section 17 Limits on use of personal information
               Omit “person.” from section 17(c).
               Insert instead—
               person, or
               (d)    the use of the information for the other purpose is to enable information to be shared
                      with and used by the Centre for Health Record Linkage.
        [2A] Section 18 Limits on disclosure of personal information
               Omit “person.” from section 18(1)(c).
               Insert instead—
               person, or
               (d)    the disclosure of the information is—
                      (i)    for a purpose other than the purpose for which the information was collected,
                             and
                      (ii)   to enable information to be shared with and used by the Centre for Health
                             Record Linkage.
