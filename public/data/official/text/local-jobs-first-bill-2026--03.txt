Local Jobs First Bill 2026

No        , 2026


A Bill for
An Act to establish the office of the Local Jobs First Commissioner and to make further provision
about the procurement of goods and services by government agencies; to amend the Public Works
and Procurement Act 1912 to make provision about the debarment of suppliers and other
consequential matters; and for related purposes.




The LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY has this day agreed to this Bill with/without amendment.

  1    Name of Act                                                                                            3

               This Act is the Local Jobs First Act 2026.                                                     4

  2    Commencement                                                                                           5

               This Act commences as follows—                                                                 6
               (a) for Parts 4 and 5, Divisions 2 and 3 and Schedule 4.2—on a day or days to be               7
                     appointed by proclamation,                                                               8
               (b) otherwise—on the date of assent to this Act.                                               9

  3    Definitions                                                                                           10

         (1)   The dictionary in Schedule 6 defines words used in this Act.                                  11

         (2)   In this Act, a reference to the value of a procurement includes an estimated value of         12
               the procurement, calculated in accordance with the regulations.                               13
               Note— The Interpretation Act 1987 contains definitions and other provisions that affect the   14
               interpretation and application of this Act.                                                   15

  4    Application of Act                                                                                    16

         (1)   This Act applies to the procurement of goods and services by or for a government              17
               agency to which the Public Works and Procurement Act 1912, Part 11 applies.                   18

         (2)   However, this Act does not apply to the following—                                            19
               (a) procurement carried out by or for a person or body if carried out in one or more          20
                   of the following ways—                                                                    21
                     (i) in the exercise of a function under the Electricity Infrastructure                  22
                           Investment Act 2020,                                                              23
                    (ii) in accordance with a direction or authorisation under that Act,                     24
               (b) procurement in relation to renewable energy infrastructure prescribed by the              25
                   regulations.                                                                              26

         (3)   The Minister must not recommend the making of a regulation under subsection (2)(b)            27
               without the concurrence of the following—                                                     28
               (a) the Premier,                                                                              29
               (b) the Procurement Minister,                                                                 30
               (c) the Minister administering the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020.            31

  5    Act to bind Crown                                                                                     32

               This Act binds the Crown in right of New South Wales and, to the extent the                   33
               legislative power of the Parliament of New South Wales permits, the Crown in all its          34
               other capacities.                                                                             35

Division 1           Appointment of Local Jobs First Commissioner                                     2

  6   Appointment of Commissioner                                                                     3

         (1)   The Governor may appoint a Local Jobs First Commissioner (the Commissioner).           4

         (2)   The Commissioner holds office for the term, not more than 5 years, specified in the    5
               instrument of appointment, but is eligible for reappointment.                          6

         (3)   A person is not eligible to be appointed for more than 2 terms of office as            7
               Commissioner, whether or not the terms are consecutive terms.                          8

         (4)   The Commissioner is entitled to be paid—                                               9
               (a) remuneration in accordance with the Statutory and Other Offices                   10
                    Remuneration Act 1975, and                                                       11
               (b) travelling and subsistence allowances the Minister may decide from time to        12
                    time.                                                                            13

         (5)   The Commissioner is subject to the control and direction of the Minister.             14

         (6)   Subsection (5) does not apply for the following purposes—                             15
               (a) the Commissioner advising the Procurement Board under section 9(1)(h),            16
               (b) the Commissioner giving a recommendation to the Procurement Minister              17
                    under section 20(2),                                                             18
               (c) the Commissioner giving advice or making recommendations to the Minister.         19

  7   Vacancy in office of Commissioner                                                              20

               The office of Commissioner becomes vacant if the Commissioner—                        21
               (a) dies, or                                                                          22
               (b) completes a term of office and is not reappointed, or                             23
               (c) resigns from office by notice to the Minister, or                                 24
               (d) is removed from office by the Governor under the Government Sector                25
                     Employment Act 2013, Part 6, or                                                 26
               (e) becomes personally insolvent, or                                                  27
                (f) becomes a mentally incapacitated person, or                                      28
               (g) is convicted—                                                                     29
                      (i) in New South Wales of an offence punishable by imprisonment for 12         30
                            months or more, or                                                       31
                     (ii) elsewhere of an offence that, if committed in New South Wales, would       32
                            be an offence punishable by imprisonment for 12 months or more, or       33
               (h) is imprisoned in relation to a conviction for an offence—                         34
                      (i) in New South Wales, or                                                     35
                     (ii) elsewhere than in New South Wales that, if committed in New South          36
                            Wales, would be an offence punishable by imprisonment.                   37

         (1)   The Minister may appoint a person to act as Commissioner during—                      39
               (a) the illness or absence of the Commissioner, or                                    40
               (b) a vacancy in the office of Commissioner.                                          41

  9   Functions of Commissioner                                                                      8

         (1)   The Commissioner has the following functions—                                         9
               (a) to promote the use of locally produced goods and services in NSW                 10
                    Government procurement,                                                         11
               (b) to promote the employment and development of local workers, including            12
                    learning workers, through NSW Government procurement,                           13
               (c) to promote the participation of local manufacturers and small and medium         14
                    businesses in NSW Government procurement,                                       15
               (d) to promote the employment and development of regional workers through, and       16
                    the participation of regional manufacturers and regional small and medium       17
                    businesses in, NSW Government procurement,                                      18
               (e) to advise and build capability of government agencies in relation to the         19
                    procurement of locally produced goods and services,                             20
                (f) to promote lawful and ethical practices by suppliers in the supply chain for    21
                    NSW Government procurement,                                                     22
               (g) to recognise suppliers and agencies that demonstrate excellence and              23
                    commitment in promoting the objectives of the local procurement policy,         24
               (h) to advise the Procurement Board on government agency compliance with the         25
                    local procurement policy,                                                       26
                (i) to promote, monitor and report on the implementation of the local               27
                    procurement policy,                                                             28
                (j) to engage with the Procurement Board and government agencies to monitor         29
                    supplier compliance with local procurement plans,                               30
               (k) to consult with the Procurement Board in relation to the local procurement       31
                    policy,                                                                         32
                (l) to consult, engage and receive information from government agencies and         33

                    the Commissioner’s functions under this Act,                                    35

                     Anti-slavery Commissioner                                                      37
               (m)   other functions conferred or imposed on the Commissioner by or under this      38
                     Act or the regulations.                                                        39

13    Local Jobs First Advisory Board                                                                  2

         (1)   The Minister may establish a Local Jobs First Advisory Board (the Advisory Board).      3

         (2)   The Advisory Board must consist of the Commissioner and not more than 8 other           4
               members appointed by the Minister, comprising—                                          5
               (a) not more than 3 members who are nominated by a peak employee                        6
                    organisation,                                                                      7
               (b) not more than 3 members who, in the Minister’s opinion, represent employer          8
                    organisations, and                                                                 9
               (c) not more than 2 members who, in the Minister’s opinion, have skills,               10
                    qualifications and experience relevant to the Advisory Board’s functions.         11

         (3)   In appointing members to the Advisory Board, the Minister must—                        12
                (a) ensure the Advisory Board has the same number of members appointed under          13
                     subsection (2)(a) and (b), and                                                   14
               (b) not appoint a member under subsection 2(a), (b) or (c) who, in the Minister’s      15
                     opinion, represents a government sector agency within the meaning of the         16
                     Government Sector Employment Act 2013, and                                       17
                (c) consider the need for the members of the Advisory Board to together have          18
                     skills, qualifications and experience relevant to the Advisory Board’s           19
                     functions.                                                                       20

         (4)   The Commissioner is the chairperson of the Advisory Board.                             21

         (5)   The procedure of the Advisory Board must be decided by the Commissioner,               22
               including—                                                                             23
                (a) the calling of meetings, and                                                      24
               (b) the conduct of business at the meetings, and                                       25
                (c) the disclosure of pecuniary interests.                                            26

         (6)   Schedule 1 contains provisions relating to the members of the Advisory Board.          27

         (7)   In this section—                                                                       28
               employer organisations means the following—                                            29
                (a) an industrial organisation of employers within the meaning of the Industrial      30
                      Relations Act 1996,                                                             31
               (b) an association of employers registered as an organisation under the Fair Work      32
                      (Registered Organisations) Act 2009 of the Commonwealth,                        33
                (c) a State peak council within the meaning of the Industrial Relations Act 1996,     34
                      section 216,                                                                    35
               (d) another organisation representing an industry.                                     36
               peak employee organisation means a State peak council within the meaning of the        37
               Industrial Relations Act 1996, section 215.                                            38

               (c) to promote the participation of local manufacturers and small and medium             21
                     businesses in NSW Government procurement,                                          22
               (d) other objectives prescribed by the regulations.                                      23

19    Suppliers to have local procurement plan                                                       21

         (1)   A government agency must not enter into a contract with a supplier for NSW            22
               government procurement of a value or kind prescribed by the regulations unless—       23
               (a) the supplier has a plan (a local procurement plan) that sets out how the          24
                     supplier will meet the relevant requirements of the local procurement policy    25
                     for the supplier, and                                                           26
               (b) the supplier has given a copy of the local procurement plan to the government     27
                     agency, and                                                                     28
               (c) the contract requires compliance with the local procurement plan.                 29

         (2)   This section does not apply to procurement to which the local procurement policy      30
               does not apply.                                                                       31

         (3)   This section does not prevent a government agency from, in other circumstances,       32
               requiring a supplier to—                                                              33
                (a) have a local procurement plan, and                                               34
               (b) give a copy of the plan to the government agency.                                 35
